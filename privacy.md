# Privacy Policy for The Umami Studio
**Last updated:** July 30, 2026

## Introduction
The Umami Studio builds privacy-first, minimalist utility applications. This Privacy Policy explains our general approach to privacy and what information we collect across our website and applications.

## Our Philosophy
We believe that your data belongs to you. Our core philosophy is to design apps that work natively and offline as much as possible. We do not track you, profile you, or sell your information.

## Website Visitors
Our website (theumamistudio.github.io) is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics, or trackers on this website to monitor your behavior. GitHub may collect basic connection information (such as IP addresses) as part of their standard hosting infrastructure.

## App-Specific Privacy Policies
Because each of our applications may have different features (e.g., using local device sensors or specific APIs), some apps have their own specific privacy policies. You can find these policies linked within the respective apps or on their specific pages on our website. 

For example, you can read the [Audio Rescue Privacy Policy](/audiorescue/privacy).

## Third-Party Services
While we avoid third-party tracking, some of our apps may rely on essential third-party services (like crash reporting to improve stability) which are strictly opt-in. Details regarding third-party services used by specific apps are documented in their respective privacy policies.
