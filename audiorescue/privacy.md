# Privacy Policy for Audio Rescue
**Last updated:** September 30, 2026

## Introduction
Audio Rescue helps you run speaker-care sessions after water exposure. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information We Store
Audio Rescue is designed to work primarily on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the core features.

The app may store settings locally on your device, including dark mode, haptic feedback, reminder preferences, advanced frequency selection, and last clean timestamp.

## Audio And Device Use
The app provides a native visual cleaning countdown, device-positioning guide, and local tone playback.

The app does not request microphone access, does not record your environment, and does not upload audio.

## Notifications
If you enable cleaner reminders, the app may request notification permission and schedule local reminders. Reminder settings are stored on your device.

## How We Use Information
Local settings are used only to provide the experience you expect, such as remembering appearance preferences and reminder configuration. We do not sell personal information or use local settings for cross-app advertising profiles.

## Third-Party Services
App stores, operating systems, and crash-reporting services may collect technical data according to their own policies. Those services are controlled by their providers, not by Audio Rescue.

We use **Firebase Analytics**, **Crashlytics**, and **Remote Config**. as third-party services. Remote Config allows us to manage app features dynamically. With your explicit consent, Analytics and Crashlytics collect screen views, custom events, crash reports, and device/app diagnostics.

## Advertising
We use **Google AdMob** and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising in Audio Rescue. Advertising helps us keep the app free. We do not control the content of the advertisements you may see, and the presence of an ad is not an endorsement of any product or service it promotes.

### Third-party advertising partners
Ads are served by Google, and may also be delivered through Google's advertising partners and other third-party ad networks. These partners may use information about your device and your app activity to select and display ads. You can learn more about how Google uses data from mobile apps in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Advertising Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### Personalized and non-personalized ads
Where permitted, we may request personalized ads, which are selected using information about your activity, such as the features you use in the app and general device information.

We use **Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to ask for your consent before requesting personalized ads. If you do not grant consent — or if you are in a region where consent cannot be obtained, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland — we request **non-personalized ads** instead, which are not based on your personal activity. You may revisit and change your choice at any time from **Settings → Privacy Options** in the app.

### Data used for advertising
When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may transmit the following to Google and its partners:
- Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
- Your device model, operating system and app version
- Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Ad interaction information, such as whether an ad was displayed, viewed, or clicked
- Performance and diagnostic information, including crash and error reports

This information is collected to serve ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and limit the frequency of ads you are shown. We do not use your activity inside Audio Rescue to build an advertising profile that is shared with third parties.

### Opting out
You can opt out of personalized ads through **Settings → Privacy Options** in the app, or through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies on [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/). Opting out does not remove ads from the app; ads will simply no longer be personalized.

## Data Retention and Your Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold, or to object to its processing. 

Locally stored data remains until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Because Audio Rescue does not create user accounts and sessions are not synced to our servers, we do not hold remote account data that we could retrieve or delete on your behalf.

## Children
Audio Rescue is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly request personalized ads for users who have indicated they are under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
